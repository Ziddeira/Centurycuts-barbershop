More than a haircut

Century Cuts is a Latino-owned barbershop on Century Blvd in Garden Grove, led by owner and master barber Chris “Century” Gonzalez. Under the hex lights you'll find barbers who take their time, listen to what you want and sweat the details — from a crisp skin fade to a full freehand design. Details make the difference.

Everyone is welcome here — walk-ins too, though appointments get priority. Kick back, catch the game, shoot a hoop and leave looking your best.
